## About
- Acme Store sells home and kitchen goods online and in one shop.
- Shop address: 123 Sample Street, Springfield.

## Opening hours
- Shop: Monday–Saturday 9:00–18:00. Closed Sundays and public holidays.
- Online orders are accepted 24/7.
- Customer team replies on Messenger Monday–Friday 9:00–17:00.

## Shipping
- Standard delivery: 3–5 business days, free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.
- Express delivery: 1–2 business days, $12.
- We ship within the country only.
- Tracking: customers get a tracking link by email when the order ships. The bot cannot look up orders; for order-specific questions, connect the customer to a person.

## Returns and refunds
- Unused items can be returned within 30 days with proof of purchase.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after we receive the item.
- Damaged or wrong items: ask the customer for a photo and connect them to a person.

## Payment
- Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and cash in the shop.
